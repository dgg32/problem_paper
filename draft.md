Papers at Risk: Retraction Prognosis for Academic Publications
==============
***Using AI and a graph of retracted papers to find risky publications***

Science moves forward by standing on the shoulders of past work. Every published paper becomes a building block for new research. But when a paper contains seriously flawed data, wrong conclusions, or deliberate fraud, it acts like a compromised Jenga block that must be quickly removed to limit its pollution of downstream science. Yet it takes a median of two years before flawed papers get retracted. During that window, they continue to accumulate citations from unsuspecting researchers. By the time an official notice appears, the damage has already propagated deep into the literature, sending other scientists down costly blind alleys.
